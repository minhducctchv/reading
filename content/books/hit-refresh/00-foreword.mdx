# Foreword
## By Bill Gates

I have known Satya Nadella for more than twenty years. We first met in the mid-1990s. At that time, I was the CEO of Microsoft. Satya was working on our server software. That software was starting to grow quickly. 

We focused on the long-term growth of the business. This approach had two main benefits. First, it gave Microsoft a new way to grow. Second, it helped develop many of the leaders who run the company today. Satya is one of those leaders.

Later, I worked closely with Satya again. He led our team to build a great search engine. At that time, we were behind Google. Our original search team had left. Satya joined the group to fix the situation. He was humble and looked toward the future. He was also practical. He asked smart questions about our plans. He worked very well with our best engineers.

Satya became the CEO of Microsoft. He immediately started making changes. The title of this book, "Hit Refresh," explains his idea. He did not throw away everything from the past. When you refresh a web page, some things stay the same. However, Satya changed the company's focus. Microsoft moved away from focusing only on Windows. 

Satya created a new mission for the company. He talks to everyone. He listens to customers, researchers, and other leaders. Most importantly, he is focusing on a few key technologies. These include artificial intelligence (AI) and cloud computing. Microsoft wants to be a leader in these areas.

This smart approach works for any company in the digital age. The technology world is very complex now. Many big companies are working on new ideas. These companies include Google, Apple, Facebook, and Amazon. There are also advanced users all around the world. The PC is no longer the only important device.

Even with these changes, the digital revolution is just starting. Artificial intelligence is a good example. We spend a lot of time on simple tasks. We manually schedule meetings and pay bills. In the future, an AI agent will help us. It will know when you have free time at work. It will help you finish your tasks. AI will make our lives more productive and creative.

Innovation will improve other parts of life too. This is a big part of my work at the Gates Foundation. We want to reduce unfairness in the world. Digital tools and genetic science are helping us. We are very close to ending polio. Polio would be the second human disease to be completely gone. 

In some countries, digital money helps people. It lets low-income users save and borrow money. In U.S. schools, software helps students learn. It allows them to study at their own speed. It helps them focus on the skills they need to improve.

Every new technology has challenges. What happens to people if AI or robots take their jobs? Will users trust AI with their personal information? If an AI could give you advice on your work, would you want it?

This is why books like "Hit Refresh" are important. Satya shows us how to use new technology well. He also answers difficult questions. He shares his own life story. He uses many literary quotes. He even shares lessons from the game of cricket.

We should be positive about the future. The world is getting better. Progress is happening faster than ever. This book is a helpful guide for an exciting future.
